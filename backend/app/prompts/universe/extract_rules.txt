###############################################
WORLD RULE DEFINITION
###############################################

A world rule is a law that consistently governs the universe.

Examples

Magic requires a wand.

Death Note kills anyone whose name is written.

Titans cannot regenerate after the nape is destroyed.

The Force can be sensed by Force-sensitive beings.

Pokémon battles follow turn-based rules.

The One Ring corrupts its bearer.
